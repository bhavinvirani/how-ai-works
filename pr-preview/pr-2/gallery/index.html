For a quantity that varies smoothly. The value is always visible, because watching a number change while something else changes is the entire lesson.

Toggle

For something that is either on or off. It reports itself as a switch rather than a checkbox — a checkbox says “include this when you submit”, a switch says “this is on right now”.
