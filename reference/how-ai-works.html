The whole thing, in one paragraph

Some problems cannot be solved by writing rules — nobody can explain how they recognise a face. So instead we build machines full of adjustable dials and let them tune themselves against examples until they stop being wrong. Everything else in this guide is a consequence of that one idea.

How to use this

It is written to be read in order — each topic assumes the ones before it and nothing else. But every topic also stands alone, and the How this connects links at the bottom of each one let you follow an idea backwards to its foundation or forwards to where it gets used.

The interactive pieces are not decoration. Several ideas here are genuinely hard to grasp from words and obvious once you have moved a slider. Play with them.
